Storage Adequacy vs. Renewable Penetration Bubble size = Total storage (BESS + PHS GWh) · Click any bubble to open country detail
X-axis: renewable electricity share (% of total generation, incl. hydro) — end-2024/early-2025 estimate. Y-axis: BESS energy (GWh) per GW of installed renewable capacity — a proxy for battery storage adequacy. Countries with high RE penetration but low Y-values typically rely on hydro reservoirs, pumped storage or interconnectors for flexibility rather than BESS. Bubble size encodes total grid-scale storage (BESS + PHS GWh). Dashed lines at 40 % RE and 0.15 GWh/GW separate quadrants. Renewable capacity estimates: IEA / IRENA 2024. Storage data: end-2024 as reported elsewhere in this Atlas.
BESS Deployment vs. Pipeline — The Race to Build Bubble size = Pumped hydro capacity (GW) · Colour = region · Click bubble to open country detail
X-axis (log scale): deployed grid-scale BESS energy (GWh, end-2024 estimate). Y-axis: BESS capacity currently under construction (GW). Bubble size encodes pumped hydro power capacity (GW); countries without PHS appear as minimum-size circles. Quadrant midlines at 3 GWh deployed (X) and 3 GW under construction (Y). Countries with zero deployed BESS are excluded from the log-scale X-axis. Data: end-2024 / early-2025 estimates as reported in this Atlas.

Storage Readiness Index — Country Rankings
Score weights: BESS GWh 30 pts PHS GW 20 pts Maturity 20 pts DR Status 15 pts Pipeline 15 pts Log-normalised · max≈100 · click row to open country
Storage Readiness Index (SRI): composite 0–100 score from five log-normalised components. BESS GWh normalised to China (168 GWh, global max). PHS GW normalised to China (58.7 GW). Pipeline normalised to China (50 GW under construction). DR ordinal: Established=4, Growing=3, Nascent=2, None=1 → mapped 0–1. Maturity ordinal: Leader=5, Advanced=4, Active=3, Emerging=2, Early Stage=1 → mapped 0–1. Weights: BESS 30 pts · PHS 20 pts · Maturity 20 pts · DR 15 pts · Pipeline 15 pts. All data end-2024/early-2025. Index methodology © S. Küfeoğlu 2026.
